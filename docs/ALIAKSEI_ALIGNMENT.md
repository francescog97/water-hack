## Product decision and boundaries

The first product is **documented verification of suspected river pollution in Bulgaria**. Its sequence is observation → anomaly assessment → potential impacts → competent-authority and legal verification → specialist approval → assigned tasks and outcome history. The report's differentiator is the connection between evidence, jurisdiction, reviewed procedure and accountable execution. Flood monitoring alone does not satisfy it.

The initial design-partner hypothesis is one River Basin Directorate, paired with one utility or industrial operator as a potential paying pilot customer. No named organisation is a confirmed partner or customer. Utilities already monitor water; the value hypothesis concerns integration and faster justified decisions.

Three distinct findings must remain separate: an observed anomaly, a possible risk, and an established violation. An approved demonstration workflow is also distinct from an official institutional decision. Satellite indicators alone establish neither a particular contaminant nor its source, offender, liability or exceedance of a legal limit.

## Release review checklist

1. Exercise a fresh pollution journey through evidence, receptors, authority/legal review, approve/reject, assign, progress, complete and timeline/export readback.
2. Try disallowed transitions, missing evidence, missing actor/reason, conflicting updates and another user's records through the API as well as the UI. Confirm review is a server-enforced prerequisite.
3. Compare visible, persisted and exported data modes, legal states, owners, timestamps and outcome evidence. Confirm demo reviewer approval cannot certify law or pollution.
4. Confirm missing/invalid sources fail visibly; no data substitution, chemistry/discharge conflation or unsupported spatial joins occur.
5. Retest the retained flood journey, source protection, private-access behaviour and four-language desktop/mobile navigation relevant to the changes.
6. Visually inspect deck renders and application screenshots. Check all implementation claims against actual check results and all public external claims against dated primary-source evidence.
7. Recheck original-input hashes, including the report. Record actual validation and any unresolved expert/customer evidence in the review handoff. Keep GitHub issue open in Review until user approval.

This audit does not certify legislation, satellite science, water quality, jurisdiction, regulatory compliance or customer demand. Those findings require the domain and organisational evidence identified above.
