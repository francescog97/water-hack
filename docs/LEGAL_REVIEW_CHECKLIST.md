# Aliaksei — five priority checks

No item below is marked completed by the implementation agent.

| Check | Research lead | Evidence required before any claim |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Municipal role and current wording | Disaster Protection Act Article 65 in the dated English copy; EC municipal overview | Current consolidated Bulgarian provision, exact clause, effective date for the event, translation checked, actor, duty vs discretion, prerequisites |
| 2. Regional coordination role | Article 64 research lead and EC regional overview | Same checks; territorial office and relationship to municipal coordination |
| 3. Basin plan relevance | MOEW FRMP 2022–2027 adoption notice and actual applicable plan | Specific plan and measure, named implementation actor, basin/gauge crosswalk, distinction between strategic planning and incident response |
| 4. Local incident procedure and deadlines | Municipal disaster/flood plan, currently not supplied | Authentic document, version, territorial scope, activation conditions, any actual deadline and whether statutory, plan-based or an internal target |
| 5. Gauge and formal declaration | Original station metadata and any authentic declaration document | Correct gauge, municipality, units/aggregation, official thresholds if any, source and period of a declaration. A demo band is never declaration evidence |

For each reviewed record capture: source URL, article/section, exact version, effective-from/to, bounded quotation or accurate paraphrase, translation status, applicability conditions, confidence, named reviewer and review date. Preserve the original unreviewed record/version in an audit trail when replacing it.

Do not infer that missing information means legislation is absent. If a question cannot be verified during the hackathon, leave it visible as an open review task.
