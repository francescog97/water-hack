# Aliaksei — pollution-first review checklist

The report-aligned demonstrator starts with suspected river pollution. The five checks below are **pending qualified review**; the app contains source leads and simulated workflow approval, not verified applicable duties. Review at least 3–5 event-relevant provisions or procedures before describing the operational legal package as validated.

| Check | What Aliaksei or a qualified Bulgarian reviewer must establish |
|---|---|
| Sample and inference | Appropriate sampling method, identity/chain of custody, actual laboratory evidence, uncertainty and competing sediment/runoff explanations. The constructed 8 → 96 mg/L TSS example is not a real finding, NTU measurement or pollutant identification. |
| Territory and institutional competence | Exact reach/coordinates, competent basin directorate and inspectorate, current remit and applicable procedure. Danube Basin Directorate and RIEW Sofia are candidate routes only. Confirm any utility/municipal role separately. |
| Current legal text and permits | Authentic consolidated Bulgarian text and exact article/section, effective dates, national implementation where relevant, permit/site scope and applicable prerequisites. The EU laboratory-quality directive is a research lead, not a standalone incident-duty determination. |
| Procedure and notification | Who is authorised to decide, required evidence, verified recipients/channel and any genuine statutory or plan-based timing. Distinguish duty, discretion and proposed internal work. The app sends no notification. |
| Follow-up and decision record | Who can approve, reject, assign, revisit and close the real case; required evidence and retention/update procedure. Demo personas, task completion and self-recorded review never establish professional credentials, pollution or legal applicability. |

For each verified entry record the primary URL/document, provision/locator, exact version and effective-from/to, territory/event/permit scope, accurate paraphrase, translation status, conditions, actor, duty versus discretion, timing classification, named reviewer/date and remaining uncertainty. Retain previous versions. Do not populate statutory deadlines or enforcement powers without this evidence.

The source register and exact local-data limitations are in [POLLUTION_DATA_LEGAL_AUDIT](POLLUTION_DATA_LEGAL_AUDIT.md); implementation gaps and the external legal-acceptance condition are in [ALIAKSEI_ALIGNMENT](ALIAKSEI_ALIGNMENT.md). A user-set task target is only an internal demonstration timestamp.

## Retained flood-module priority checks

No item below is marked completed by the implementation agent.

| Check | Research lead | Evidence required before any claim |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Municipal role and current wording | Disaster Protection Act Article 65 in the dated English copy; EC municipal overview | Current consolidated Bulgarian provision, exact clause, effective date for the event, translation checked, actor, duty vs discretion, prerequisites |
| 2. Regional coordination role | Article 64 research lead and EC regional overview | Same checks; territorial office and relationship to municipal coordination |
| 3. Basin plan relevance | MOEW FRMP 2022–2027 adoption notice and actual applicable plan | Specific plan and measure, named implementation actor, basin/gauge crosswalk, distinction between strategic planning and incident response |
| 4. Local incident procedure and deadlines | Municipal disaster/flood plan, currently not supplied | Authentic document, version, territorial scope, activation conditions, any actual deadline and whether statutory, plan-based or an internal target |
| 5. Gauge and formal declaration | Original station metadata and any authentic declaration document | Correct gauge, municipality, units/aggregation, official thresholds if any, source and period of a declaration. A demo band is never declaration evidence |

For each reviewed record capture: source URL, article/section, exact version, effective-from/to, bounded quotation or accurate paraphrase, translation status, applicability conditions, confidence, named reviewer and review date. Preserve the original unreviewed record/version in an audit trail when replacing it.

Do not infer that missing information means legislation is absent. If a question cannot be verified during the hackathon, leave it visible as an open review task.
